About Teegreen One-Way ANOVA Calculator

Teegreen One-Way ANOVA Calculator is a free online statistical tool designed to help students, researchers, educators, and professionals analyze experimental data by comparing the means of multiple treatment groups.

Developed by Teegreen Tech, this tool simplifies one-way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) by calculating Mean ± SD, ANOVA tables, F-calculated, F-tabulated, P-value, Least Significant Difference (LSD), and significance groupings using superscript letters.

Our mission is to make statistical analysis simple, accessible, and reliable while helping users perform scientific data analysis efficiently and reduce manual calculation errors.

The calculator is designed to support research and experimental studies in fields such as fisheries, agriculture, biology, food science, and other scientific disciplines where comparison of treatment means is required.

We are committed to developing practical digital tools that support education, research, and data analysis while providing an intuitive and user-friendly experience.

Features

One-Way ANOVA:
Compare the means of multiple independent treatment groups and determine whether significant differences exist.

LSD Test:
Perform post-hoc mean comparison and identify significant differences between treatments using superscript letters.

Statistical Output:
Generate Mean ± SD results, ANOVA tables, F-values, P-values, and statistical conclusions.

Export Results:
Save analysis results in a structured Excel format for documentation and reporting.
